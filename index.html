FAQ[05]

Questions,
answered.

Do I need to install anything on my laptop?

No. In Bluetooth mode your phone pairs as a standard Bluetooth mouse and keyboard, which Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS support out of the box.

Which phones work?

Android 9 or newer. A few manufacturers disable the Bluetooth keyboard/mouse feature; the app tells you if yours does. iPhone doesn't allow apps to act as a Bluetooth mouse.

Will keep-awake work with my phone locked?

Yes. It runs in the background with an ongoing notification you can stop at any time. For long sessions, keep the phone charging.

Why doesn't my laptop see the phone as a mouse?

Pair while CtrlDesk is open on the Bluetooth screen. If you paired earlier, remove the phone from the laptop's Bluetooth list and pair again.

Does CtrlDesk collect any data?

No. There's no account, analytics or server. See the privacy policy.
