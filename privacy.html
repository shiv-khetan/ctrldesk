Privacy policy[Updated 23 Oct 2026]

We collect
nothing.

CtrlDesk ("the app") lets your Android phone control your own laptop as a Bluetooth mouse and keyboard, or over your local Wi-Fi with an optional helper program. It is built so that your data never leaves your devices.

Information we collect

None. CtrlDesk has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal or usage data.

Information stored on your phone

  • The Bluetooth address of the last laptop you connected to, so the app can reconnect automatically.
  • The Wi-Fi helper's address and pairing PIN, if you use Wi-Fi mode.
  • Text snippets you save and your presentation-app choice.

This information stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Information sent to your laptop

Mouse movements, clicks and key presses travel directly from your phone to the laptop you paired with, over Bluetooth or your local network. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to act as a mouse and keyboard and to be visible while pairing.
  • Notifications: to show that CtrlDesk is connected or keeping your laptop awake.
  • Network: only for the optional Wi-Fi mode on your local network.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking libraries.

Children

CtrlDesk is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
